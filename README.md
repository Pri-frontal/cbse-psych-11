# CBSE Class 11 Psychology: Interactive Exemplar & Problem-Solving Guide 🧠📚

> **Congratulations, you're a human photocopier.**  
> Mugging up definitions is easy; applying them to real-world human behavior requires actual grit. If you're looking for textbook answers to copy-paste, flip the next page of your reference book. If you want to stop freezing on high-order case studies and actually master scenario problem-solving, start here.

---

## 📌 Project Vision
Standard textbooks give you the background—they are your main meal. But hit with tricky scenarios and suddenly memorizing definitions is not enough.

This guide acts as a practical learning companion to help you build real mental muscle: Training course includes:
- **Scenario Cases:** Situation-based problems that force you to spot psychology in action, not just on paper.
- **Interactive Practice Drills:** Decision-tree exercises, quick matching games, and instant-feedback quizzes to test your reasoning in real time.
- **Tiered Question Banks:** Step-by-step problem sets that start with core application and build up to high-level evaluation and debate.

## 🛠️ How Our Questions Work (3-Tier Framework)
Every topic in this guide is structured to build your confidence step-by-step:

1. **Tier 1 (Core Application):** Apply textbook definitions to concepts hidden in everyday examples.
2. **Tier 2 (Case Study Analysis):** Analyze realistic responses, human behaviors, and observational situations.
3. **Tier 3 (Debate & Evaluation):** Critically evaluate psychological theories, common myths, and research methods.
